Precision Dexa “The Ripple Effect” Community Nomination Contest: Official Rules

Last updated October 29, 2026.

These are the official rules for The Ripple Effect.

  1. Sponsor. Precision Dexa, Suite 14, 19550 SE Brady Rd, Camas, WA 98607 (inside CLASH Beauty Collective). Contact: welcome@precisiondexa.com.

  2. Not affiliated with Meta. This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, or administered by, or associated with, Instagram or Facebook/Meta. By participating, each nominator, nominee and voter fully releases and holds Meta Platforms, Inc. harmless from any liability. Questions, comments or complaints about this promotion go to the Sponsor, not Meta.

  3. No purchase necessary. No purchase, payment, waitlist signup, follow, like or share is required to nominate, vote or win, and none of these improves anyone's chances. Any offers shown on this page, including founding-member packs, are separate from this promotion.

  4. Promotion period. Nominations open Sunday, November 1, 2026 at 12:00 AM Pacific Time and close Sunday, November 22, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT. Finalists will be posted on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, November 26, 2026. Voting runs from the time the finalist post is published until Monday, November 30, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT. Nominations and votes received outside these windows will not count.

  5. Who can be nominated. Nominees must be 18 or older and live in Clark County, Washington. Precision Dexa owners, employees and contractors, and their immediate family and household members, are not eligible to be nominated or to win. Void where prohibited.

  6. How to nominate. During the nomination period, complete the nomination form at precisiondexa.com/ripple with your name and email, the nominee's name and town, and a few words on how they are making a difference in our community. As an alternate method of entry, you may instead leave a comment on The Ripple Effect post on the Precision Dexa Facebook page or @precisiondexa on Instagram with the nominee's name and why; form and comment nominations are treated equally. You may nominate more than one person, but only once per nominee across both methods; repeat nominations of the same person from the same nominator count once. Nominations from fake, duplicate or automated accounts or submissions are void. Please do not post anyone's phone number, address or other private details in a public comment. The Sponsor may hide or remove comments that are offensive, off-topic or include private information.

  7. Finalist selection. After nominations close, the Precision Dexa team will review all eligible nominations and choose three finalists based on the positive impact on our community described in the nominations. The number of times someone is nominated does not decide who becomes a finalist. Each potential finalist will be contacted privately and must agree before their name, story or photo is shared. If a potential finalist declines, cannot be reached in time, or is ineligible, the team will choose another nominee.

  8. Voting. On Thanksgiving Day, the Sponsor will post the three finalists, numbered 1, 2 and 3, on its Facebook page and Instagram account. To vote, comment the number of your chosen finalist on the finalist post. You do not need to follow either account to vote. One vote per account; if an account votes more than once, only its first vote counts. Votes from Facebook and Instagram are added together. Votes from fake, duplicate or automated accounts, or votes obtained by offering anything of value, are void. If there is a tie, the Precision Dexa team will choose the winner from the tied finalists.

  9. Winner and notification. The finalist with the most valid votes wins. The winner will be announced on the Sponsor's Facebook page and Instagram account on or about Tuesday, December 1, 2026, and contacted privately by the Sponsor using the contact details provided or by direct message from the official Precision Dexa page or account only. The Sponsor will never ask for payment, a card number, or a password. The winner must reply within 72 hours with their name and contact details for scheduling. If the winner does not respond in time, is ineligible, or declines, the prize goes to the finalist with the next-most votes.

  10. Prize. One (1) DEXA Body Composition scan at Precision Dexa, Camas, WA. Approximate retail value: $149. The studio is opening soon; the prize is redeemable once it opens. It must be scheduled and used within 90 days of the studio's public opening date, after which it expires. The prize has no cash value and is not transferable (except as described in Section 11), exchangeable or redeemable for cash. No substitutions, except that the Sponsor may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Nominators and voters do not receive a prize. The winner is responsible for any taxes.

  11. Scan eligibility. A DEXA Body Composition scan uses a low dose of X-ray and requires an order from a licensed healthcare provider. If the winner does not have one, the studio's nurse practitioner can provide an order when appropriate. The winner must complete the studio's standard intake and safety screening (for example, scans are not performed during pregnancy), and the scan table has a weight limit of 350 lb. If the winner cannot be scanned safely, the Sponsor may let them transfer the prize to an eligible adult family member, at the Sponsor's sole discretion. A DEXA Body Composition scan is not a diagnostic test and does not replace medical advice.

  12. Publicity. Finalists' names, stories and photos are shared only with their permission. With the winner's permission, the Sponsor may share the winner's name, city and story on its social channels. Nominators' names are not shared publicly without their permission.

  13. How we use information. Nominator names and emails are used only to run this promotion and to contact nominators about their nominations. A nominator's email is added to the Precision Dexa waitlist only if they leave the waitlist box checked on the form (and confirm by email); waitlist members can unsubscribe at any time. Nominee details (name, town, story and any contact details provided) are used only to review nominations and contact finalists, are never added to a mailing list, and are deleted within 90 days after the promotion ends, except for the winner's and finalists' details needed to deliver the prize. We do not sell any information collected through this promotion.

  14. General. The Sponsor may disqualify anyone who tampers with the promotion, uses fake accounts, or breaks these rules. If the promotion cannot run as planned, the Sponsor may cancel or modify it. By taking part, nominators, nominees and voters agree to these rules and to the Sponsor's decisions, which are final. Participants release the Sponsor and Meta from claims arising from participation or from the prize.

  15. Winner's name. For the winner's name and city, email welcome@precisiondexa.com within 30 days after the promotion ends.
