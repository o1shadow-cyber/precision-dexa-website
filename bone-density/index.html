Questions

Bone density scan questions

What is included in a diagnostic bone density scan?

A radiologist report, bone mineral density measurements, T-scores and Z-scores when applicable, and whether bone density falls within expected ranges for the appropriate comparison group. A qualified healthcare provider interprets the results.

How is this different from a body composition scan?

A body composition scan is $149, early-customer price $96.85, and estimates fat mass, lean mass, and visceral fat for wellness tracking. A diagnostic bone density scan includes a radiologist report and measures bone mineral density for clinical evaluation. Pricing coming soon. One does not replace the other.
