Complete Health Assessment questions

What is included in the Complete Health Assessment?

A body composition scan — fat mass, lean mass, body fat percentage, and a visceral fat estimate — and a diagnostic bone density scan with a radiologist report, including T-scores and Z-scores when applicable. Body composition results are measurements, not a diagnosis. Bone density results need interpretation by a qualified healthcare provider.

How does the Complete Health Assessment price compare with booking each scan alone?

A single body composition scan is $149, early-customer price $96.85. A diagnostic bone density scan includes a radiologist report. Pricing coming soon.
