Big Dreams, Beautiful Spaces & Exciting Progress!

A dream is becoming a reality, and we're excited to bring you along for the journey!

We are thrilled to share a little glimpse of the beautiful space that will soon be home to Precision Dexa! Located inside CLASH Beauty Collective, our space is part of a beautiful environment designed to give small businesses the opportunity to grow while providing their clients with a welcoming, comfortable experience.

🌿 Making Progress, One Step at a Time

Behind the scenes, our Precision Dexa space is coming together as we prepare to welcome our very first clients. While we eagerly await the arrival of our DEXA machine, we are continuing to prepare our space and get everything ready for opening day.

Our goal is to create more than just a place to get a scan. We want Precision Dexa to be a welcoming, comfortable environment where you can relax and learn more about your bone health and body composition, better understand your numbers, and feel empowered with information about your health.
