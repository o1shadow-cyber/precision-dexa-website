Questions

Complete Health Assessment questions

What is included in the Complete Health Assessment?

A body composition scan — fat mass, lean mass, body fat percentage, and a visceral fat estimate — and a diagnostic bone density scan, with T-scores and Z-scores when applicable. Body composition results are measurements, not a diagnosis. Bone density results need interpretation by a qualified healthcare provider.

How does the $249 price compare with booking each scan alone?

A single body composition scan is $149. A single diagnostic bone density scan is $199 and includes a radiologist report. The Complete Health Assessment includes both for $249.

Is the Complete Health Assessment the same as the 2-Pack Body Composition Scan?

No. The Complete Health Assessment is body composition plus diagnostic bone density for $249. The 2-Pack Body Composition Scan is a separate package of body composition scans only on Jane, also $249, and the 3-Pack Body Composition Scan is $349. Available through Dec 31, 2026. Each package expires 52 weeks after purchase.

Who performs the assessment?

Amber, a Registered Radiologic Technologist with 18 years in imaging, performs every scan. Precision Dexa was founded by Amber, RT.
