About Precision Dexa

Why We Started Precision Dexa.

For over a decade, I worked as a Registered Radiologic Technologist, running DEXA scans inside hospital walls — measuring bone density, body composition, the real numbers behind someone's health. And every time, I had the same thought: why does this only happen after a referral, a diagnosis, a waiting list? The accuracy was always there. Access wasn't.

Precision Dexa exists to change that. Same clinical-grade equipment, same RT-certified precision, no referral required — just walk in, get scanned, and finally know what your body is actually made of.

Based right here in Camas, built for anyone who wants real data instead of guesswork.

— Amber, RT

RT-certified. Locally owned and operated in Camas, WA.
