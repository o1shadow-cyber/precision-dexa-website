What is a Body Composition Scan?

A Body Composition Scan uses low-dose X-ray (DXA) technology to give you a detailed picture of what your body is made of. Your bathroom scale tells you how much you weigh. This scan shows what that weight actually is. It's quick and comfortable. Just wear comfortable clothes without metal.

What it measures

  • Fat mass: total body fat, including where it's stored
  • Lean mass: muscle, organs and other lean tissue
  • Visceral fat: an estimate of the fat around your internal organs
  • Bone mineral content: a general look at the mineral in your bones. For a clinical bone density test, our Diagnostic Bone Density scan includes a radiologist report.

Why it matters

Two people can weigh the same and have very different bodies. Knowing your numbers lets you track real changes in fat and muscle over time, see beyond the number on the scale, and make informed decisions about your health goals. Amber will walk you through your results in plain language.

Know your numbers. Understand your body.
