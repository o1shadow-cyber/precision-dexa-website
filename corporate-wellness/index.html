Questions

Corporate wellness questions

How many people can you scan in a day?

Up to 20 scans in a single day. Appointments run back to back, about 15 minutes each.

When is group pricing available?

Group pricing applies for groups of 10 or more. Contact the studio for a proposal. Individual scans are listed separately: body composition $149, diagnostic bone density $199 (includes a radiologist report), and the Complete Health Assessment $249.

Are results shared with the employer?

No. Individual reports go to the participant only, not to the employer.

Where are group scan days held?

At Precision Dexa, 19550 SE Brady Road, Suite 14, Camas, WA 98607. Group days are for Camas and Vancouver-area employers, gyms, and teams, including Washougal, East Vancouver, and Cascade Park.

How do we schedule a group day or book one scan?

Email welcome@precisiondexa.com or call 360.606.3117 to request a proposal. Individuals can book a scan online on Jane. The soft opening is December 1, 2026.
